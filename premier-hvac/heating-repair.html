Heating & Furnace Repair

Desert nights drop into the 30s. We keep furnaces and heat pumps ready before winter reminds you.

Furnaces and heat pumps

We service gas furnaces, electric heat, and heat pumps, including the dual-fuel setups common in newer valley homes. No heat, short cycling, strange smells, or rising gas bills are all worth a visit.

Safety first

Every heating visit includes a heat exchanger inspection and carbon monoxide check. A cracked heat exchanger is a replace-the-furnace conversation, and we will show you exactly what we see.

Fall tune-ups

A $129 fall tune-up catches most winter failures early: burners cleaned, ignition tested, gas pressure verified, and airflow balanced.
