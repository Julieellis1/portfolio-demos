AC Installation & Replacement

A new system sized right for your home and for 115-degree summers. Installed in a day, permitted, and hauled away.

Right-sizing matters more than brand

An oversized unit short-cycles, never dehumidifies, and dies early. We run a proper load calculation on your home, ductwork, insulation, and sun exposure, then recommend the size your house actually needs.

What installation day looks like

We protect your floors, remove the old equipment, set the new condenser and air handler, braze and pressure-test the lineset, pull a deep vacuum, charge to spec, and commission the system. Most homes are done by late afternoon.

Rebates and financing

High-efficiency systems often qualify for utility rebates and federal tax credits. We handle the paperwork and offer financing plans so the monthly payment can be less than the energy savings.
