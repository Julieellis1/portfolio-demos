Indoor Air Quality Solutions

Dust storms, wildfire smoke, and construction dust are a Vegas reality. We make the air inside your home the clean part of your day.

Whole-home filtration

Media cabinets with MERV-13 or higher filtration catch the fine dust that standard 1-inch filters miss. We size the filter to your system so airflow stays strong.

UV and purification

UV-C lights at the coil stop mold growth where Vegas humidity spikes, and whole-home purifiers neutralize airborne particles and odors.

Duct inspection and sealing

Leaky ducts pull attic dust straight into your living room. We inspect, seal, and verify with airflow measurements.
