The Premier Comfort Plan

Two tune-ups a year, priority scheduling, and 15% off repairs. For less than a dollar a day.

What each tune-up includes

Full system inspection, coil cleaning, refrigerant level check, electrical connection tightening, capacitor testing, drain line flush, thermostat calibration, and airflow measurement. About 60 to 90 minutes of real work, with a written report after.

Plan perks

Priority same-day scheduling, 15% off all repairs, no overtime fees ever, waived diagnostic on plan visits, and reminders so you never miss a tune-up.

Why twice a year

Vegas dust clogs a condenser in months, not years. A spring cooling tune-up and a fall heating tune-up keep efficiency high and catch small failures before July or January turns them into emergencies.
