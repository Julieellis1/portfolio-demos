Our story

Built for neighbors, not shareholders

We started Premier because we kept hearing the same stories: a tech who upsold a new system for a $40 part, a four-day wait in a 110-degree heat wave, a bill that doubled between the quote and the invoice.

So we built the company we wished existed. Two technicians who answer the phone, show up when promised, explain what failed in plain English, and charge the price we quoted. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and their neighbors, which tells us the formula works.
