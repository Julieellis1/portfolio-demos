AC Repair in Las Vegas

When the Vegas sun is doing its worst, a broken AC is an emergency. We treat it like one.

What we fix

Compressors, capacitors, contactors, refrigerant leaks, frozen coils, blower motors, thermostats, and drainage problems. If it has a wire or a refrigerant line, we have fixed it.

How a visit works

We arrive in a two-hour window, diagnose the system end to end, and show you the failed part and the flat-rate price before any work begins. Most repairs finish on the same visit because the van carries the parts that fail most.

Our repair warranty

Every repair carries a 2-year labor warranty on top of the manufacturer parts warranty. If our fix fails within two years, we come back free.
