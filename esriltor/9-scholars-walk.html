About this home

A contemporary three-storey townhouse overlooking the New Islington marina, offered unfurnished and available from 1st November.

The ground floor has a dining kitchen opening to a private terrace, the first floor a bright dual-aspect living room with marina views, and the top floor three bedrooms with three bathrooms. Residents parking and secure cycle storage are included.

New Islington is ten minutes on foot from the Northern Quarter and Ancoats, with the marina, Cotton Field Park and the Metrolink all close by. An ideal base for professionals who want modern space near the city buzz.
