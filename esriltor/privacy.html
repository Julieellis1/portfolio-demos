Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Esriltor Estate Agents, 24 Burton Road, Didsbury, Manchester M20 2LR, is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. Contact us at hello@esriltor.co.uk with any privacy questions.

What we collect

When you use our valuation, viewing or contact forms, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property address and the details of your enquiry. We also collect basic technical information such as your browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site working well.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to your enquiry, arrange valuations and viewings, and manage any instruction you give us. We will never sell your data. With your permission, we may occasionally email you about relevant homes; you can opt out at any time.

Who sees it

Your information is seen only by our team and the trusted services that help us run the business, such as our email provider and website host. We share details with solicitors, surveyors or other agents in a chain only where needed to progress your sale or purchase, and only with your knowledge.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for 12 months. If you instruct us to sell or let your property, we keep records for six years to meet legal and anti-money-laundering obligations, then delete them securely.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to marketing, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing hello@esriltor.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
