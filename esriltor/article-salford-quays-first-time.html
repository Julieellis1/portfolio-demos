Buying guide

First-time buyer? Start with Salford Quays

September 20265 min readBy Priya Nair

If you are buying your first home in Manchester, you have probably done the maths on Didsbury or Chorlton and winced. Before you stretch to breaking point, look at the Quays.

The deposit maths works

A two-bedroom apartment at the Quays currently sells between £250,000 and £350,000. At 10 per cent deposit, that is £25,000 to £35,000, roughly half what the same deposit looks like on a south Manchester terrace. Your monthly mortgage payment lands comfortably below the rent on an equivalent flat.

The safety net is built in

Here is the part most first-time buyers miss: rental demand at the Quays is relentless. MediaCity professionals, young couples, sharers. If your plans change in three years, your flat lets in days, not weeks. That rental cushion makes lenders comfortable and keeps your options open.

What to watch for

Check the lease length (anything under 150 years deserves a conversation), read the service charge history, and buy the best view you can afford: water-facing flats hold their premium. Come and see a few with us before you decide. The tram ride from the Quays to town takes 18 minutes, which usually settles it.
