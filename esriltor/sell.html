How it works

From valuation to sold, in six steps

1

Free valuation

A senior valuer visits, brings three comparable sales from your street, and gives you an honest number. No obligation, no pressure.

2

Photography and floor plan

Twilight photography, measured floor plans and a 3D tour, all included. Your home listed on every major portal within 48 hours.

3

Accompanied viewings

A negotiator who knows your property walks every viewing. We gather feedback after each one and share it with you.

4

Offers, negotiated properly

We qualify every buyer on finance and chain position before presenting their offer, then negotiate firmly on your behalf.

5

Chain management

Weekly calls to solicitors, surveyors and everyone up and down the chain. Most sales stall here. Ours do not.

6

Completion day

We confirm funds, coordinate key release, and stay on the phone until you tell us the money has landed.
