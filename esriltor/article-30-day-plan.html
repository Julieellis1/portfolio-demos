Selling guide

The 30-day plan for selling before Christmas

September 20266 min readBy James Okafor

Every autumn we meet sellers with the same goal: under offer before Christmas, moved by spring. It is absolutely achievable, but only with a plan. Here is the exact 30-day sequence we run for motivated sellers.

Days 1 to 7: prepare, do not list yet

Declutter ruthlessly, fix the small things buyers photograph mentally (dripping taps, scuffed paint, tired sealant), and get the garden tidy. We book photography for day seven at the latest. Twilight shots in October and November are worth their weight: your home glows while the street goes dark.

Days 8 to 14: launch with intent

List on a Thursday. Portals refresh, weekend viewing searches peak, and your home appears at the top of new listing alerts. Price it on this month's comparable sales, not last spring's. An honest price in week one beats a reduction in week six, every time.

Days 15 to 30: viewings and decision

We accompany every viewing and report feedback within 24 hours. If three weekends pass with views but no offers, something needs to change, usually the price or the lead photograph. By day 30, a well-priced Manchester home should be under offer. If yours is not, we sit down together and reset the plan honestly.
