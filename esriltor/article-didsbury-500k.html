Market update

What £500,000 buys you in Didsbury this autumn

October 20264 min readBy Sarah Whitfield

Half a million pounds is a serious budget in Didsbury, but what it buys has shifted this year. Terraces, the backbone of the area, are holding firm. Flats, after a long run of strength, have softened by around five per cent. If you are buying this autumn, that split matters.

The terrace market is still competitive

A well-kept three-bedroom Victorian terrace on the roads off Burton Road or Didsbury village now changes hands between £475,000 and £550,000. The best-presented homes still attract multiple offers in the first fortnight. Anything sitting longer than a month is usually priced on last year's numbers, and sellers are having to adjust.

Where the value is

Look one street back from the prime roads. Palatine Road and its neighbours command the premium, but the roads running parallel often offer the same size house for £30,000 to £50,000 less, with the same schools and the same walk to the village. New-build apartments around the village centre are where negotiation room genuinely exists: developers are offering incentives rather than cutting headline prices, so ask about stamp duty contributions and furniture packs.

Our advice for autumn buyers

Get your mortgage agreed in principle before you view, move fast on terraces, and take your time on flats. And if a terrace has been on the market six weeks, make the offer the seller was hoping to avoid. This is the season when realistic pricing wins.
