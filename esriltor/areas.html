Areas we know street by street

We sell a tight patch and we sell it well. Here is an honest read on each area, with the numbers to back it up.

Didsbury

Avg. sold price £512,000

Our home patch. Victorian terraces, village high street, outstanding schools and the Metrolink into town in 15 minutes. The most competitive market we cover: well-priced homes here still attract multiple offers.

Chorlton

Avg. sold price £438,000

Independent cafes, bars and shops, two parks, and a tram that reaches town in 20 minutes. Character cottages and roomy terraces draw young families who want village life without leaving the city.

Salford Quays

Avg. sold price £289,000

Waterside apartments with MediaCity on the doorstep. Strong rental demand from young professionals makes this our top pick for first-time buyers who want their money to work hard.

View homes at the Quays →

Altrincham

Avg. sold price £624,000

Market town on the Cheshire fringe with grammar schools, a famous food market and some of the finest family streets in Greater Manchester. Premium prices, but they hold.

City Centre

Avg. sold price £356,000

Penthouses, lofts and warehouse conversions for people who want to walk to everything. Best suited to cash buyers and investors: leasehold details matter here, and we read every lease before we list.

New Islington

Avg. sold price £398,000

Modern townhouses around the marina, ten minutes on foot from the Northern Quarter. A young neighbourhood still finding its feet, which is exactly why the value is here.
