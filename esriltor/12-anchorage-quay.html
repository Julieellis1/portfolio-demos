About this home

Apartment 12 sits on the third floor of Anchorage Quay with a private balcony looking straight over the water. Inside, an open-plan living room and kitchen runs the full depth of the flat, with floor-to-ceiling glazing that fills the space with light.

Both bedrooms are proper doubles, and the principal bedroom has its own en suite. The building offers a residents concierge, secure allocated parking, and landscaped communal gardens down to the waterfront path.

Salford Quays has grown into one of Greater Manchester's most convenient places to live: the tram reaches the city centre in under 20 minutes, MediaCity is on the doorstep, and the Lowry, watersports centre and a string of bars and restaurants are all within walking distance.
