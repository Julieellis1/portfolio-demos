About this home

Set back behind a walled front garden on one of Didsbury's most admired roads, 14 Palatine Road is a handsome Victorian terrace that keeps its period character while working hard as a modern family home.

The ground floor opens from a tiled entrance hall into a bay-fronted living room with its original fireplace, then through to a dining room and a kitchen that runs the full width of the house with doors straight onto the garden. Upstairs, four bedrooms and a family bathroom are joined by a converted cellar that now works beautifully as a home office or playroom.

The 60ft rear garden faces south west and catches the evening sun, with a paved terrace, lawn, and mature borders. Palatine Road sits moments from Didsbury village's bars and independent shops, with highly regarded schools and the Metrolink within easy reach.
