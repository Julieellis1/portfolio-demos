An independent agency, on purpose

Esriltor started in 2009 with one office, one phone line, and a stubborn belief: that selling homes is a local craft, not a call-centre process.

Our story

Twelve people. One patch. Zero scripts.

We have stayed deliberately small. Every negotiator at Esriltor sells the same streets they live on, which means valuations grounded in reality, viewings led by people who can answer the awkward questions, and advice we would give our own family.

Seventeen years in, most of our work comes from recommendations and repeat clients. We think that says more than any award.

What we stand for

Three rules we never break

Tell the truth about price

An overvalued home costs you months and money. We show our working with comparable sales, and we will talk you out of a fantasy figure even when it costs us the instruction.

Answer the phone

You will always reach a person who knows your file. No ticket queues, no "your call is important to us". Our clients have our direct numbers.

Chase the chain

A sale is not done at offer stage. We call solicitors, surveyors and agents up and down the chain every week until completion day. It is unglamorous, and it works.
