About this home

On the top floor of the landmark No.1 Deansgate, Penthouse 3 offers nearly 2,000 sq ft of lateral living with floor-to-ceiling glass on two sides and sweeping views across the city skyline.

Offered furnished to a high standard, the penthouse has three double bedrooms each with en suite, a vast open-plan living and dining space, and a separate snug ideal as a home office. A 24-hour concierge, two secure parking spaces and a private residents gym complete the package.

Available now on a long-term tenancy. One of Manchester's most recognisable addresses, moments from Spinningfields, King Street and the city's best restaurants.
