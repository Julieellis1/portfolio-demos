About this home

Behind electric gates on one of Altrincham's premier roads, 7 Woodlands Road is a substantial detached family home set in generous landscaped grounds.

The ground floor centres on a kitchen diner with a marble island and bi-fold doors opening to the garden, alongside a formal living room, a study, and a utility room. Upstairs, five bedrooms include a principal suite with dressing room and en suite, served by three further bathrooms.

Outside, a block-paved driveway leads to a double garage, while the rear garden offers terraces, lawns and mature planting with a high degree of privacy. Woodlands Road is within the catchment for Altrincham's celebrated grammar schools and a short walk from the town centre and Metrolink.
