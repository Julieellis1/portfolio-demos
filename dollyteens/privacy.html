Privacy

Your privacy matters.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. DollyTeens Kiddies College respects the privacy of students, parents, guardians, staff and website visitors.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how the website is used, information may include names, contact details, admission enquiry information, communication preferences and information voluntarily submitted through forms.

Children's Information

Because the school serves children, student information is handled with particular care and only collected, used, retained and shared for legitimate educational, administrative, safety or legal purposes.

How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, process admissions, communicate with families, administer school services, maintain records and improve our website.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Your Rights

Parents and guardians may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about their family, subject to legal and educational record requirements.

Contact

Privacy enquiries: contact details available on request.

This is general website information, not legal advice. The final policy should be reviewed for the school's jurisdiction and actual data practices before publication.
