Activities

More than lessons.

Learning, Activities & Student Experience

A child's school experience should develop knowledge, confidence, creativity, friendships and responsibility.

Reading & Literacy

Stories, reading practice, vocabulary, writing and speaking activities that build confident communicators.

STEM & Discovery

Questions, experiments, patterns, technology and real-world problems through age-appropriate activities.

Creative Arts

Art, craft, music, drama and performance give children room to express ideas and grow in confidence.

Sports & Movement

Physical activities that support coordination, teamwork, discipline and enjoyment of active living.

Clubs & Enrichment

Reading, coding, science, art, music, public speaking, chess and cultural activities to explore.

Character Development

Courtesy, responsibility, cooperation, self-management and leadership practised through everyday routines.

School Events

Throughout the year, children shine at cultural days, sports days, exhibitions, graduation ceremonies, open days and academic showcases. Families are warmly invited to celebrate with us.
