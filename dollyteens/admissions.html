Admission Process

Five simple steps to joining the DollyTeens family.

Learn About DollyTeens

Our admissions team shares programme information, expectations, fees and available places.

Where applicable, your child joins an age-appropriate interaction or assessment.

Admission Decision

Families receive the next steps and required documentation.

Complete Registration

Submit the required forms and complete registration before the agreed start date.

What Parents May Need

Having these documents ready makes registration smooth. Our admissions team will confirm the exact requirements for your child's class.

  • Child's birth certificate or official birth record
  • Previous school report, where applicable
  • Passport photographs
  • Parent or guardian identification
  • Completed admission forms
  • Medical or emergency information required by the school
