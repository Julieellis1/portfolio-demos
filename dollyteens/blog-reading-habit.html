Helping Children Build a Daily Reading Habit

Simple ways families can make reading a natural part of everyday life.

Start small and stay consistent

Ten minutes of reading together each day builds more than an occasional long session. Choose a regular moment, after dinner or before bed, and protect it the way you would any important appointment.

Let them choose

Children read more when they have a say. Offer two or three age-appropriate books and let your child pick. Comics, picture books and joke books all count.

Read aloud, even when they can read alone

Hearing fluent reading builds vocabulary and comprehension. Take turns reading pages, or read the tricky parts while your child reads the rest.

Talk about the story

Ask what they think will happen next, or which character they would like to meet. Conversation turns reading into thinking.

Keep books within reach

A small basket of books in the living room or bedroom invites browsing. Visit the library regularly and let your child borrow freely.
