About Us

A school built around the whole child.

Our Story

Children Learn Best When They Feel Safe and Inspired

DollyTeens was founded around a simple belief: children learn best when they feel safe, valued and inspired to participate. Our approach brings together clear academic expectations, engaging classroom experiences, character development and strong communication with families.

We create learning experiences that help children ask questions, express themselves, solve problems and develop the confidence to take on new challenges.

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating and caring education that equips children with knowledge, confidence, good character and the skills to navigate an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, curious and responsible young people who are prepared to learn, contribute and lead with integrity.

Our Core Values

The character we nurture every day, in every classroom.

Respect

We treat ourselves and others with dignity.

Integrity

We encourage honesty and responsible choices.

Curiosity

We ask questions and remain open to discovery.

Excellence

We take pride in effort, improvement and achievement.

Kindness

We look for ways to support and encourage others.

Responsibility

We take ownership of our learning and actions.

Our Educational Approach

How learning happens at DollyTeens.

  • Strong Foundations. We focus on the essential knowledge and skills children need for future learning.
  • Active Participation. Children discuss, investigate, create, practise and explain, not simply listen.
  • Individual Attention. We recognise that learners progress differently and provide support and appropriate challenge.
  • Character Through Everyday School Life. Good character grows through routines, relationships and responsibilities.

A Partnership With Parents

Parents and guardians are important members of the learning community. We communicate clearly about progress, school activities, expectations and opportunities to support learning at home.
