Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents usually ask before joining DollyTeens. Still curious? We love questions.

Our Early Years programme welcomes children from age 2, and our Primary programme runs from Primary 1 to Primary 6. The right class for your child depends on their age and previous schooling, which we discuss during the admission process.

Start by making an enquiry through our admissions page or by contacting the school. Our team will share programme information, fees and available places, then arrange a school visit or age-appropriate assessment for your child.

Typically a birth certificate or birth record, previous school reports where applicable, passport photographs, parent or guardian identification, completed admission forms and any medical or emergency information the school requires.

Transport arrangements vary by term and route. Please contact our admissions team for current transport options in your area.

School hours are shared during the admission process. Our team will confirm the exact schedule for your child's programme.

We keep families informed about progress, activities and expectations through regular updates, meetings and open communication with teachers and the school office.

A school bag, water bottle, lunch where applicable, and a cheerful heart. We will share a full checklist for your child's class before resumption.

We recognise that learners progress differently and provide support, encouragement and appropriate challenge. Please discuss your child's needs with us so we can plan together.
